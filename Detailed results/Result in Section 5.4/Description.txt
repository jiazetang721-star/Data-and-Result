kappa_x.pkl and kappa_y.pkl store optimal built CCs and RFs. They are two dimension lists. The first dimension represents the size of uncertainty sets (kappa) from 0.01 to 0.20. For example, kappa_x [0] stores the optimal CCs with kappa = 0.01.norm_x.pkl and norm_y.pkl store optimal built CCs and RFs. They are three dimension lists. The first dimension represents the size of uncertainty sets (kappa) from 0.05 to 0.15. The second dimension represents norm-type for uncertainty set in the sequence of L_1, L_2 and L_inf. For example, kappa_x [0,0] stores the optimal CCs with kappa = 0.05 and norm type is L_1.The figure is plotted with python.
